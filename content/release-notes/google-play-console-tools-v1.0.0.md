# 🎨 Google Play Console Tools

**Complete developer toolkit for preparing Android applications for Google Play Console store listing submission.**

Generate high-res 512x512 app icons, validate screenshot dimensions, and fix aspect ratios automatically &mdash; all from the command line.

### ✨ Key Capabilities
- 📱 **Icon Generator**: Converts any source image into standard 512x512 PNG icons meeting Google Play store listing guidelines.
- 📐 **Screenshot Formatter**: Automatically scales and paddings screenshots to official Play Store aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16, 18:9, etc.).
- 🖼️ **Feature Graphic Creator**: Generates 1024x500 promotional banners with background gradients and mockups.
- ✅ **Store Listing Validator**: Pre-flight audit tool to check all assets before uploading to the Google Play Console.
