## QuickDash v5.2.1

### Downloads
- **`app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`** — 64-bit ARM for modern devices (2020+)
- **`app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk`** — 32-bit ARM for older devices
- **`app-universal-release.apk`** — works on all supported devices
- **`app-release.aab`** — Google Play App Bundle

### Install
1. Download the APK for your device.
2. On Android, enable **Settings → Install unknown apps** for your browser.
3. Open the file and tap **Install**.
